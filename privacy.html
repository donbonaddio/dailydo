Privacy Policy

Daily Do · Last updated November 2026

Daily Do works on your iPhone. There is no Daily Do account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. We never receive your calendar, reminders, email, health data, or anything else. Daily Do gathers your day on your own phone, builds your page there, and keeps nothing longer than it needs to.

Calendar and Reminders

With your permission, Daily Do reads the calendars and Reminders lists on your iPhone to show today’s and the coming week’s appointments, your chosen shopping list, and anything due. It only reads them and never changes them. This all happens on your iPhone.

Email (optional)

If you choose to connect email, Daily Do connects directly from your iPhone to your provider’s mail server (for example Gmail, iCloud, Yahoo, AOL or Outlook.com) over an encrypted connection. There is no server of ours in between.

  • How you sign in. For most providers you create an app password in your provider’s account settings and paste it into Daily Do. For Outlook.com and Hotmail you sign in with Microsoft in Apple’s secure sign-in window; Daily Do receives a sign-in token, never your Microsoft password. If you use another provider, Daily Do may look up its mail server settings by your email domain (for example “comcast.net”) in Mozilla’s public directory; only the domain is sent.
  • Read-only. Your inbox is opened in read-only mode. Daily Do never sends, deletes, moves, labels, or marks any message as read.
  • Only what the page needs. For recent inbox mail (the last 1–3 days, your choice), it reads the sender, subject, date, whether it’s unread, and the first few lines of the message for a one-line preview. It does not download attachments.
  • Not stored. Email is held in memory only while your page is being built. It is never saved by the app, apart from what appears on your latest page (see below).
  • Your sign-in stays on your phone. Your email address and app password or sign-in token are kept in your iPhone’s Keychain, on that device only, never synced to iCloud, and sent only to your mail provider. Disconnect email in Settings to delete them, or revoke the app password or Daily Do’s access in your provider’s account at any time.

Apple Health (optional)

If you allow it, Daily Do reads a few Health measures to summarize your day: sleep, steps, active energy, exercise minutes, resting heart rate and heart rate variability, workouts, weight, calories, water and mindful minutes. It only reads; it never writes to Health. Health data stays on your iPhone: it is never sent anywhere, never used for advertising, and never shared. Daily Do shows information only and gives no medical advice.

Weather and places

To show the forecast, Daily Do asks Apple Weather (WeatherKit) for the weather at the places you choose, and uses Apple’s map service to turn a town or ZIP code into a location. These requests go to Apple and carry no personal information beyond the place.

News and headlines

Headlines come from publishers’ public news feeds, Hacker News, and Google News. To show local news and topics you follow, Daily Do sends Google News the town or ZIP code, or the topic words, you chose. These requests contain no personal information and no cookies. As with any website, these services can see the internet address a request comes from. Tapping a story opens it on the publisher’s site, which has its own privacy policy.

Daily summary

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, a one-line summary of your day is written by Apple’s on-device model. It runs on your iPhone; nothing is sent to us or to any AI service.

Your page

Daily Do keeps only the latest copy of your page (a PDF) on your iPhone, replacing it each time it rebuilds. It leaves your phone only when you choose to Print or Share it, and then it goes only where you send it. A short version of your day is shared with Daily Do’s widgets and, if you have one, your Apple Watch, so they can show it.

Notifications

The morning reminder is a local notification scheduled on your iPhone. It shows counts only (“3 things on the calendar today”), never appointment names, so nothing private appears on your lock screen.

Settings

Your preferences, such as calendar categories, the shopping list, places, news topics, email category rules, and the morning time, are saved only on your iPhone.

Links to other apps

Tapping an item opens Apple’s Calendar or Reminders, your mail app or provider’s website, or a news site. Those apps and sites have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

Disconnect email in Daily Do’s Settings to remove your email sign-in. Deleting the app removes everything Daily Do kept on your iPhone. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Daily Do is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
